# Glossary

**Flowgate / constraint.** A transmission line or group of lines with a maximum amount of power it can safely carry.

**Binding.** A flowgate is binding when it is at its limit. No more power can flow through it, so the grid has to use more expensive generation on the receiving side.

**Shadow price.** What the limit is costing, in dollars per megawatt-hour. It is how much cheaper power would be if the line could carry one more megawatt.

**LMP (locational marginal price).** The price of power at a specific place on the grid. When no line binds, LMPs are nearly the same everywhere. When one binds, they split.

**Spread.** The difference between the LMPs at two locations. This is what traders try to predict.

**Rule of thumb.** The expensive side of a binding line is where the power wants to go. The cheap side is where it is stuck.

**Hour-ending vs hour-beginning.** Some PJM series label an hour by when it ends, others by when it starts. Join them without checking and every relationship is shifted by an hour.
